The Tashu approach

We treat your skin, not just the symptom on the day.

That line comes from the studio itself, and guests repeat it back in review after review. Skin is analysed first — several clients mention a skin camera and a proper consultation — then a plan is built around your concern and your budget, across as many sittings as it genuinely needs.

Questions

Before you visit

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended, and sometimes required at busy times, but the studio does accept walk-ins. Calling ahead on +91 91230 38218 is the surest way to get your preferred slot.

How many sittings will my skin need?

It depends entirely on the concern. Reviewers commonly mention courses of three to six sittings for acne, pigmentation and brightening. Tashu assesses your skin first and recommends a plan — several guests say she’ll tell you honestly if you don’t need something.

Is the salon women-only?

It’s a ladies salon and aesthetics clinic, and multiple reviewers highlight that it is exclusively for women and respects privacy. It identifies as women-owned and LGBTQ+ friendly.

What does it cost?

Prices aren’t published and vary by hair length, skin concern and number of sittings. Reviewers repeatedly describe the pricing as reasonable and affordable, but for an accurate quote it’s best to call the studio directly.

How do I get there?

60A, Park Street (Mother Teresa Sarani), Mullick Bazar, in the Park Street area of Kolkata — postcode 700017. There’s free street parking and on-site parking, and the entrance is wheelchair accessible.

What are the opening hours?

Open every day from 12:00 noon to 10:30 pm, per the studio’s Google listing. Hours can differ on public holidays, so a quick call is wise if you’re travelling in.
